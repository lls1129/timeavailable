Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

timeavailable is a small tool for finding a meeting time that works for a group. It has no user accounts and collects as little as possible. This page explains exactly what it handles.

What the service stores

  • Polls: the title and description the host writes, the dates and time slots offered, the timezone, and the final time the host picks.
  • Responses: the name a participant types, the time slots they mark as available or "if needed", and, if they choose to set one, a one-way hash of their optional password (the password itself is never stored).
  • Access keys: random tokens that let the host administer a poll and let a participant edit their own response. They are kept in your browser's local storage and sent only to this service.

Poll data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Durable Objects with SQLite storage) and kept until the host deletes the poll. Anyone with a poll's link can see the names and availability submitted to that poll; share links only with the people you intend.

Google Calendar

The optional Fill from Google Calendar button lets a participant pre-fill their availability from their own calendar. It works like this:

  • It requests a single, narrow Google permission: calendar.freebusy ("View your availability in your calendars").
  • Your browser asks Google's free/busy endpoint for the busy time ranges in the poll's dates. No event titles, descriptions, attendees, locations or other event details are ever requested or received.
  • The comparison with the poll's slots happens entirely in your browser. The resulting selection is shown to you, and only what you then choose to save is sent to this service. The busy ranges themselves and your Google access token are never sent to or stored by this service.
  • The access grant is revoked immediately after the lookup. Nothing calendar-related is stored anywhere.

timeavailable's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the service does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking scripts. No cookies.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of data to third parties. The only third parties involved are the hosting provider (Cloudflare) and, if you use the calendar button, Google, which you talk to directly from your browser.
  • No access to anything beyond what is described above. The page is served with a Content Security Policy that prevents it from contacting any other server.

Your choices

  • Participants can edit or remove their own response at any time by signing in with the same name.
  • The host can remove any response, close the poll, export it, or delete it entirely from the poll's admin link. Deleting a poll permanently removes all of its responses.
  • You can revoke the calendar permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, although the app already revokes it after each use.
